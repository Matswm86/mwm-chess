## 3D chess pieces — "0014_ Medieval HRE-ERE Chess Set" by Average3DmodelEnjoyer

The twelve piece models (`app/src/main/assets/models/{w,b}{p,n,b,r,q,k}.glb`) are
derived from **"0014_ Medieval HRE-ERE Chess Set"** by **Average3DmodelEnjoyer**,
obtained from Sketchfab. One piece of each type and colour was taken from the set,
recentred, rescaled per type to fit the board, and exported with its textures
reduced to 512 px; the geometry is otherwise unchanged. Silver plays White, gold
plays Black.
